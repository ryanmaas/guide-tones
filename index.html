Two ways to learn

Timed drill rotates through your selected chords every 5 seconds by default: 3 seconds to recall, 2 seconds to check. Change the pace or keep answers visible. Difficult, new, and due cards receive more weight; previously rated cards can still appear for extra practice. Unrated cards do not change the review schedule.

Spaced review shows due cards first, then new cards. The answer stays hidden until you reveal it. Rate your recall to schedule the next review. When every selected card is scheduled for later, you’re done for now.

Preview your way

Before starting a timed drill, use Preview: shown / hidden (Z) to choose whether each new preview card reveals its answer. The choice is saved, and toggling it also updates the current preview. Reveal (X), immediately to the right of Next, shows the current answer without changing that default. Use C or the right arrow for the next card. These controls never rate a card. Preview preferences do not expose answers in spaced review or change the timed-drill answer setting.

This is a local, Anki-inspired scheduler with adjustable ease factors, not Anki’s FSRS algorithm or a connection to Anki. Button labels show the actual next interval.

Read the keyboard

The keyboard spans three octaves, C3–B5. Use the Both column to apply a display to all marked notes, or choose separate Root and Tone styles. Key display offers highlighted keys, dots, key outlines, edge bands, hanging tags above, direct labels and hanging tags below. Tags point to the corresponding keys and stagger when adjacent notes need more room. The root is black and marked R; thirds and sevenths are blue. Dots have white outlines and white labels while the underlying keys keep their normal colors. Major intervals are marked 3 and 7, minor intervals m3 and m7, and diminished sevenths d7. Each tone appears three times. Turn off Show root before reveal (V) to hide root markers along with the answer in preview, timed drills and spaced review. Revealing an answer always shows the root again, regardless of this preference. Scroll horizontally on small screens to see the full keyboard. Notes use their chord spelling, so C♭ falls on the B key, E♯ on F, and B𝄫 on A. Switching flat / sharp roots keeps the same pitch-class cards and their review history.

The pool covers dominant 7, major 7, minor 7, minor 7♭5 (half-diminished 7), diminished 7, minor-major 7, augmented 7 and augmented-major 7. Only the first three qualities are selected by default. Use Choose chords to add the others. A diminished seventh uses m3 and d7; a minor 7♭5 uses m3 and m7. Some qualities share the same 3rd and 7th but differ in their fifth. Tap any piano key to hear a synthesized tone, or use Hear tones for the root, third and seventh.

Your progress stays here

Ratings and selections are saved in this browser on this device. Keep the HTML at the same location and open it in the same browser. Export progress to back it up or move it elsewhere. Private browsing, clearing site data, or moving the file may make saved progress unavailable. The app needs no account or internet connection.

Background tabs pause automatically. Opening these dialogs pauses the timer; use Resume when you’re ready. Shortcuts are disabled while editing settings or using a dialog.
